# Reference bar for the Macro direction

These are the techniques the Alma page must reach. They come from the four sites the client named. Each line is a commitment that the review checks.

**Source:** written from prior knowledge of the sites. The campus firewall blocks them, so check each one on a hotspot before the final review and correct anything that's wrong.

## What makes the references award-level

| Reference | What carries it |
|---|---|
| **The Sea We Breathe** | Underwater mood with real cinematography: graded colour, light shafts from above, suspended particulate at several depths, slow pacing. The camera moves; the UI stays out of the way. |
| **Igloo Inc** | One continuous 3D world with an intro sequence. The camera choreography is the navigation. Material quality (ice, glass, refraction) is the brand, and the sound design matters. |
| **Lusion v3** | A physical, tactile 3D scene that reacts to the cursor with weight. Custom post-processing (bloom, depth, grain). Minimal UI floating over a living scene. A loading moment treated as a show. |
| **Lando Norris** | A confident type system with personality and very few elements, each tuned (hover, press, transition). Video and 3D are integrated, not decorated. |

## The commitments, in priority order

1. **One authored image.** The first viewport reads as one cinematic shot, not a layout: subject, light, depth, grade. Removing the UI should leave a frame worth screenshotting.
2. **Depth of field.** There are real focal planes. The bell is in focus; near particles and far kin are out of focus, as bokeh. Focus moves with intent: focusing the email field pulls focus.
3. **Light from above.** Light shafts and caustics come from the surface, and the creature is lit by them as well as by its own glow. Its translucency reads as tissue, not as a glowing wireframe.
4. **The camera is alive.** A slow drift, a breathing response to the cursor, and a choreographed move for the key moments (the opening descent, the signup pull-back). The page itself never jumps.
5. **The grade.** A deep black point, a heavy vignette, zero-mean film grain, slight chromatic fringing toward the edges, and violet bioluminescence against near-black.
6. **Minimal, precise UI.** Subtitle-scale type, one inline email pill, tiny corner chrome. Every element gets hover, press and focus states.
7. **An intro as a show.** The light comes up as the camera sinks to the creature (under 2s, skippable, never blocking the email field).
8. **Sound, opt-in.** A low underwater bed plus soft responses to typing and signup, off by default and remembered.
